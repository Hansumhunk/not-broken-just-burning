Three things to check before sending

  1. Is this urgent? A child's immediate needs, safety, and required deadlines come before a generic pause.
  2. Is this necessary? You may not need to answer every accusation or emotion.
  3. Can I stand behind this tomorrow? Favor observable facts and a reasonable next action.

Safety and legal restrictions matter. Do not use a template to bypass no-contact orders, legal advice, agreed communication rules, or safety plans. If communication may provoke danger or retaliation, seek appropriate professional support before replying.
