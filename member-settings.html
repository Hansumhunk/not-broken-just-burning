Data boundary

What should stay local by default?

Unsaved reflection stays outside the member archive. Highly sensitive therapy/medical information, legal evidence, and private journals should not be treated as ordinary account data.

Reasonable future account data: authenticated member ID, membership tier, product/event entitlements, display name, Path focus/progress, selected low-sensitivity preferences, course/event progress, and timestamps needed to operate the account.

Rule: upgrading membership must never silently convert private reflection into server data.

Account controls later

Deletion, export, billing, and authorization must be real, not decorative.

When the backend is connected, account deletion, data export, session management, saved-work sync, membership state, purchases, and privacy controls need server-side behavior that matches the buttons the member sees.
