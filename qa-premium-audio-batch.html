How this saves time

  1. Download several finished MP3s into one folder on your computer.
  2. Select those files together using Choose Files.
  3. Use Assign consecutive pages for a sequential batch, then spot-check the previews and titles.
  4. Click Upload verified batch once. The tool sends the files directly to our private bucket and reports the results.

The narration provider currently gives us separate download links. This tool removes the repetitive renaming and uploading; it doesn't yet make AI Doc Maker export a ZIP of recordings. Professional audio rights and final editorial review are still required before public release.
