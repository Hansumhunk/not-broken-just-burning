## Additional payment incident response
- Webhook verifies additional payment session against the order's exact SKU/price/intent/charge, and records it separately if canonical session is already bound. No extra entitlement, no reassignment, no automatic refunds. Session and intent uniqueness prevent double registration. Concurrent ambiguous first binding relies on unique canonical ledger plus provider retries; if no canonical session is bound, escalate; never guess.
- `admin-cli.ts reconcile-extras ORDER_UUID` (read-only) scans all matching sessions under bounded cap. With --apply, ticket/operation UUID and operator alias, records noncanonical paid/refunded/disputed snapshots and review flag. No money movement. Confirm canonical session from ledger, verify additional payments via sandbox Dashboard, seek approval for manual refunds, verify successful refund before closure. Unresolved additional payments block new or reused purchase reservations for that buyer/SKU, while existing valid canonical entitlement stays intact. Pending refund is unresolved.
- `recover-canonical ORDER_UUID` can recover the already-bound canonical paid order only once all other sessions are verified expired or fully refunded, and every extra paid session has a matching resolved observation. Existing audited recovery transaction checks stale order snapshot and competing rights. Ambiguous unbound canonical, scan cap, incomplete payment evidence, open sessions or unresolved conflicts require human investigation. No operation silently deletes financial records or moves money.
- If Stripe event retries fail, use actual provider replay in sandbox; record a restricted ticket. Do not fabricate signed events outside fixture tests. Duplicate gross and refunded amounts remain in finance exposure report even after resolved. Future periodic operational job should reconcile snapshot drift; not created here.
