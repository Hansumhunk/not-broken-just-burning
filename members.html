What comes next

This is the member foundation, not the finished account system.

Available now in this development build: a device-local Flamewalker snapshot, Path focus, Guardian Code practice, and direct member routes into the tools and stories.

Phase 2 requires a real backend: secure login, member profiles, cross-device progress, member-only resources, account deletion/export, and carefully designed community features.

Private reflection content will not automatically become server data just because accounts exist. Sensitive journaling should remain local by default unless a member deliberately chooses otherwise.

Membership without hierarchy theater

Belonging should create access and practice, not artificial superiority.

Flamewalker remains the identity for someone doing the work. Guardian remains a future service and leadership role with responsibilities, not a shiny rank for collecting internet points.
