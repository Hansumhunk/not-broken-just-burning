Safety first

Immediate danger or crisis?

If you or a child are in immediate danger, contact emergency services. If you need domestic violence or crisis support, reach out to qualified real-world help before using a worksheet. Do not wait for a reply from NBJB.

Free · First steps checklist

Make the next 24 hours manageable.

Choose what applies. This is a planning aid, not a legal deadline calculator.

01

Check safety

Identify a safe place, emergency contacts and support for you and your children if needed.

02

Know your obligations

Locate orders, notices and deadlines, and confirm what they mean with counsel. Follow applicable orders.

03

Preserve records

Keep originals of records you are permitted to access in a secure location. Do not alter, delete or improperly obtain records.

04

Communicate deliberately

Keep necessary messages factual and child-focused. Do not threaten, retaliate or violate contact restrictions.

05

Protect your daily foundation

Plan food, sleep, work, transportation, medications and children's routines. Identify one person to ask for concrete help.

06

Choose one next action

Select one achievable task, then stop organizing when the next useful step is clear.

H.A.W.K. · High-Conflict Divorce Survival System

Make the external chaos manageable.

Adapted from the original H.A.W.K. manuscript: four phases of lawful preparation, accurate records, independent observations and disciplined action. Knowledge is for self-protection and accountability, not retaliation.

H · HARD EVIDENCE

Record what happened

Record dates, firsthand facts, sources, context and unknowns. Keep original records lawfully and distinguish observation from interpretation.

Read Timeline Before Theory →
A · AFFIDAVITS & ALLIES

Build a reliable support structure

Identify qualified counsel, trusted people and independent firsthand sources. Prepare questions for counsel; obtain legal guidance for formal statements.

Use the Six Sacred Questions →
W · WITNESS IMPACT STATEMENTS

Distinguish observation from interpretation

Record your own observations and their practical impact. Let independent witnesses describe what they personally saw in their own words, without coaching or pressure.

Explore the Pattern Map →
K · KINETIC STRATEGY

Choose actions that serve your future

Prioritize safety, obligations, professional advice, communication and child stability. Avoid escalation or retaliation.

Privacy matters

Keep case records in your control.

This center does not collect case records or accept uploads. Keep sensitive legal, family, financial and witness information offline in a secure location you control. Existing Flamewalker tools keep private writing on your device by default; avoid entering identifying case details. Do not submit sensitive records to NBJB.
