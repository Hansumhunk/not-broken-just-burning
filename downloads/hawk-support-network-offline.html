Support Network Map

Identify the help you need, the appropriate role and one respectful next action. A network is not a loyalty roster or a witness list.

Safety before planning: In immediate danger or a life-threatening emergency, call local emergency services (911 in the United States). In the U.S., call or text 988 for emotional or suicide crisis support. Outside the U.S., use appropriate local emergency and crisis services. This offline tool cannot monitor safety, notify anyone or dispatch help. Do not use it as an emergency safety plan or substitute for medical, clinical or legal care.

Use resource aliases only. No addresses, private contact details, credentials, confidential records or children's identifying information. No uploads, accounts, network or automatic storage. JSON exports and printouts are unencrypted; use a secure device and storage location.

Add a support need or arrangement

Start with the need, not a person's loyalty. Respect consent, availability, restrictions and professional roles. Confirm arrangements independently before relying on them. A gap may have no identified resource. Confirmed arrangements require an actual agreement note; resolved entries require an outcome. Dates are personal planning dates, not reminders or legal deadlines.

Your working support map

Entry counts describe a working plan, not distinct people, relationship quality, loyalty, safety or guaranteed availability.

Import replaces the current collection after confirmation. Export includes all entries, even when filtered; print includes displayed entries. Clear does not delete saved files. Closing or reloading loses unsaved work.
