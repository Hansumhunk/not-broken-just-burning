How The Forge works

Separate what happened from the story your mind built around it.

The Forge is a structured reflection exercise for moments when a thought feels absolutely true but may contain several different ingredients: observable facts, assumptions, predictions, emotional reasoning, remembered patterns, and missing information.

You are not trying to talk yourself out of a real concern. You are testing the thought hard enough to see which parts survive examination. By comparing evidence that supports and challenges the thought, then separating pattern recognition from proof, you can rewrite the conclusion into something more accurate without pretending the emotion was meaningless.

A boundary worth keeping

This is reflection, not crisis care.

If a thought involves immediate danger, self-harm, harming someone else, or a medical emergency, use real-world emergency or professional support rather than trying to reason your way through it alone.
