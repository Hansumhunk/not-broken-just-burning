Member promise

Membership should feel complete.

Ordinary digital lessons and tools should not become a string of microtransactions. Flamewalker+ is intended to include the vast majority of NBJB's self-guided digital ecosystem as it grows.

Paid-member advantage

15% off eligible extras.

When NBJB adds eligible separately sold physical products and live experiences, Flamewalker+ members are intended to receive 15% preferred pricing. Eligibility and checkout rules will be defined before those products launch.

What may still be separate

Charge separately when the value or delivery is genuinely separate.

Physical Goods

Printed books, workbooks, journals, merchandise, and other products with real production or fulfillment cost.

Live Experiences

Ticketed workshops, seminars, retreats, events, and other experiences that require live delivery, space, staffing, or substantial additional work.

High-Touch Programs

Future facilitated services or unusually large standalone programs may carry separate pricing when their delivery materially exceeds the standard self-guided membership.
