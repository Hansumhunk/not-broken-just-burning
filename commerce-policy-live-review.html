Know what you're buying.

Draft for founder review only. This page is not an effective offer and does not authorize live checkout. Review accessibility, tax, cancellation and refund language before release.

Products, price and delivery

Not Broken Just Burning (NBJB), a U.S.-based education and personal-development business, proposes individually purchased downloadable educational workbooks and toolkits. The first candidate, The Boundary Builder Workbook v1.4 (NBJB-BB-001), is a $14.99 USD one-time purchase containing a 43-page reading PDF and a 42-page printable PDF in a single private ZIP. Both PDFs are non-fillable. Any applicable transaction tax and the total due must be shown before a buyer confirms payment. Other editions and products are separate purchases, and nothing is shipped physically. The precise legal seller name, business registration and tax responsibilities still require confirmation before these draft terms become effective. For this initial Boundary Builder offer, an account in free Flamewalker is required to retain ownership and retrieve downloads; Flamewalker+ paid membership is not required and is not included. A standalone purchase is not a subscription; it does not automatically enroll a buyer in Flamewalker+ or create recurring payments.

Purchased editions are delivered only after payment has been verified by our payment provider and recorded in your NBJB account. A payment confirmation screen alone is not proof that delivery has been completed. Once ownership is recorded, the purchased edition should appear in My Library. Downloads use short-lived private links; do not share links with anyone else. If a checkout completes but your download does not appear, do not pay again: contact support with the account email and order reference so we can investigate the payment and restore eligible access or provide an appropriate refund. Each product is an edition-specific ZIP containing the stated reading and print-edition PDF files. PDFs are non-fillable; you may print selected pages or complete exercises in a separate notebook. Nothing is shipped physically.

License and availability

A completed payment grants a nonexclusive, nontransferable right to use and print the specific purchased edition for personal use. It does not transfer copyright. Do not redistribute, resell, upload the files publicly, or share account credentials. Lawful exceptions remain intact. Keep a personal backup of the downloaded files. We intend to offer re-downloads while NBJB maintains the service and the order remains in good standing, but do not promise perpetual hosting, indefinite availability, or free future editions. If your account or file access fails, contact support with the order reference.

Payment, cancellation, and purchase recovery — proposed

Boundary Builder v1.4 is a one-time digital purchase, not a recurring subscription. You can cancel before submitting payment by leaving Stripe Checkout; an abandoned, expired, or unsuccessful payment does not grant ownership or entitle you to the files, and should not be represented as a successful charge. If you believe a charge occurred but the order is pending, missing, or duplicated, do not pay a second time. Contact support with the free account email, approximate transaction date and Stripe/NBJB order reference, if available; never include card numbers or sensitive workbook answers. NBJB will reconcile the provider payment and internal order state before restoring the correct edition's access or making an authorized refund determination. Orders remain associated with the purchaser's verified free account, independently of any separate Flamewalker+ subscription. If your account becomes inaccessible, use its normal recovery process; ownership is not transferred merely by providing an email address.

Refunds and payment issues — proposed

Under the proposed policy, a buyer may request a full refund of their first purchase of an individual edition within fourteen calendar days of payment by contacting billing@notbrokenjustburning.com. A customer does not have to send their private workbook responses, disclose why they were dissatisfied, or prove that they did not download the file. The founder has authorized preparation of this proposed policy; qualified legal/compliance and operational review and an effective terms version remain required. This voluntary policy does not limit applicable statutory or payment-provider protections. Duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions and paid-but-undelivered products require prompt investigation, payment-provider reconciliation and an appropriate remedy consistent with applicable law and provider protections; do not tell customers to make a second purchase to fix missing delivery. Refunds normally return to the original payment method, subject to the processor's timeline. A completed full refund ordinarily ends future access to that edition; files already downloaded cannot be recalled. Partial refunds and disputes are reviewed individually. Your unrelated product purchases and your separate Flamewalker+ subscription are unaffected unless expressly required by a specific resolution. No automatic cancellation or refund of a paid membership occurs when you return a workbook.

Education, safety and scope

These materials offer educational reflection, practical organization and personal-development exercises, not licensed therapy, medical diagnosis, mental-health treatment, legal advice, representation, custody advice or emergency assistance. The H.A.W.K. Complete Toolkit is an optional printable binder companion; its separate purchase does not restrict any of the existing free H.A.W.K. tools. Do not disclose private information about children, co-parents, legal cases or medical histories in a checkout or support message. For urgent safety or medical concerns, contact appropriate emergency or professional services rather than using these products as crisis assistance.

Data and payments

Stripe processes payment cards; NBJB does not request or store card numbers. NBJB's secured backend maintains the account identifier, purchased edition, order/price/tax/terms version, provider references, payment state, purchase date and refund status as needed to fulfill, support, audit and reconcile transactions. Short-lived private delivery links and ownership checks protect downloads. We do not require sensitive personal journals or case files to purchase a product. We retain some financial records where legally required; support can assist with account-access, privacy and deletion requests. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.

Geography, taxes and support

The initial standalone product offering is intended for customers in the United States, subject to confirmation of seller tax nexus, product classification and any required location restrictions before the first sale. A Florida-only sales-tax conclusion must not be applied to buyers in other states. Availability and required tax collection must be configured and confirmed before the first sale; no statement here waives tax obligations. The final Checkout displays any calculated sales tax before the customer authorizes payment. For ZIP/PDF access and account delivery help, contact support@notbrokenjustburning.com. For billing corrections or refunds, contact billing@notbrokenjustburning.com; the contact page also explains the support routes. Do not email bank details, full card numbers or sensitive documents. For accessibility or download assistance, include only the order reference, account email and a brief description of the issue. Do not purchase the same edition again while support investigates a missing delivery.
