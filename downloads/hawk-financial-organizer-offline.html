Financial Organizer

Organize what you know about money, what remains uncertain and what needs qualified review. Do not mix cash activity, plans and point-in-time positions.

Privacy and accuracy: This tool never connects to banks or sends data. Never enter credentials, full account numbers, private identifiers or complete statements. Keep original records separately. Exports and printouts are unencrypted; use a suitable secure device and storage location. This is an editable working organizer, not a reconciled ledger, tax calculator, valuation, disclosure form, legal entitlement or professional advice. Obtain qualified help for important decisions. Do not conceal assets or disregard obligations.

Add a financial entry

Amounts are nonnegative magnitudes, not signed debits or credits. Unknown amounts must remain blank; zero means a known zero. Use a consistent review period and currency. Supported currencies use two decimal places; no conversion. Actual cash activity is received income or paid expenses, not invoices or outstanding balances. Transfers, shared obligations and discrepancies stay separate. Position basis is for assets/liabilities. Information-only entries are not cash activity.

Your financial review

Import replaces entries after confirmation. Export contains every entry, regardless of filters; print contains displayed entries and displayed summary only. Source reference sets are not exported.

Displayed amount groups

Grouped by period, currency, basis, category and amount status. No combined total, currency conversion, net worth or property division. Unknowns are counted, not added as zeros. Duplicate and overlapping records are not detected; review sources yourself.
