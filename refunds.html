Digital Membership Billing

No mystery charges.
Clear cancellation.

Proposed recurring-subscription cancellation and refund policy for Flamewalker+ digital memberships.

Editorial draft — not yet in effect.

These proposed subscription policies are being reviewed before paid memberships launch. They are not live purchase terms until approved and published.

1. Know what you're purchasing

Flamewalker+ is a subscription to a growing, self-guided digital learning library. The checkout screen shows the membership plan, amount, and renewal interval before payment. The proposed plans are $14.99/month and $149/year, plus any applicable taxes. An annual plan is billed once per year, not once per month. Planned future content does not have a guaranteed release schedule.

2. Automatic renewal

Paid memberships renew automatically at the selected monthly or yearly interval unless canceled before the next charge. Stripe processes payments and may email receipts or billing notices. If payment cannot be collected, your access may change according to the billing status and any recovery period communicated to you.

3. How to cancel

When paid membership is available, sign in at Flamewalker Account Access, open Membership, and use Manage Billing to open Stripe's Customer Portal and cancel future renewal. You can also request assistance by emailing notbrokenjustburning@gmail.com from the email linked to your membership.

Under the intended cancellation model, your paid digital access continues until the end of the already-paid billing period and the account then returns to the free Flamewalker tier. Canceling renewal does not delete your free account. To avoid another charge, cancel before your next scheduled renewal; allow processing time for email requests.

4. Proposed first-purchase refund option — awaiting owner approval

Proposed policy, not yet adopted: A first-time Flamewalker+ subscriber may request a refund within 7 calendar days of the initial subscription charge by emailing the support address above with the account email and charge date. If approved, the refund returns through the original payment method and paid access may end after the refund is issued.

Requests after this window and renewal payments would ordinarily not receive an automatic prorated refund merely because the subscriber forgot to cancel, subject to applicable law and any remedy NBJB offers for billing errors or significant non-delivery. The operator must approve or revise these terms before publishing the policy and launching payments.

5. Billing errors and exceptional circumstances

If you notice a duplicate charge, suspected unauthorized payment, processing error, or a substantial failure to deliver access you paid for, contact notbrokenjustburning@gmail.com with the account email, approximate charge date, and a brief explanation. Do not email full card numbers, passwords, government IDs, or other credentials. We will review the transaction and applicable legal requirements. The absence of a voluntary refund does not override consumer rights provided by law.

6. Subscription support and policies

For general subscription terms see Terms of Service. For account and local reflection data handling see Privacy & Data Use. For account problems, billing questions, or support use Contact NBJB.
