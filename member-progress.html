Privacy boundary

Saving history is explicit.

Opening a tool does not automatically copy private writing into member history. A reflection is added only when the tool offers a clear Save to My Work action and you choose it. Themes, context, outcomes, action follow-up, Pattern Lens classifications, and review takeaways are also member-entered. This release stores that history only on this device.

Cloud-sync rule: member history should require a deliberate sync choice, deletion controls, and clear separation from highly sensitive therapy, medical, legal, or private journal material.
