# Not Broken Just Burning — YouTube Distribution Architecture

Status: internal planning document. Keep behind the curtain until the channel has a coherent starter library.

Channel: https://www.youtube.com/@NotBrokenJustBurning

## Role of YouTube

YouTube should become a public discovery and preview arm of NBJB, not the full instructional library and not a separate brand competing with the website.

The relationship should be:

**YouTube discovers / previews → Free Flamewalker opens the organized starter learning library → Paid Flamewalker deepens the learning system → Flagship programs concentrate the work**

The website remains the structured home of the intellectual property. The public channel can carry movement introductions, trailers, excerpts, clips, and selected promotional teaching. Full instructional lessons intended for the member learning system should require at least Free Flamewalker access.

An unlisted YouTube URL is not reliable member authorization. Protected lessons should eventually use a delivery method capable of authenticated or signed access.

## Masculine Restoration Video System

The six anchors create the first repeatable flagship video series:

1. **Steadiness — Keep Your Footing Under Pressure**
2. **Direction — Rebuild Purpose After Life Changes**
3. **Strength — Power Without Losing Control of It**
4. **Protection — Boundaries Without Possession**
5. **Responsibility — Accountability Without Shame**
6. **Brotherhood — Rebuilding Connection Without Dependency**

Public anchor clips or trailers should point viewers toward the matching public webpage/tool and the Free Flamewalker account. Full anchor video lessons should live inside the member learning library unless a specific lesson is deliberately designated public.

## Specialized Branch Playlists

As the library grows, organize videos into life-context playlists that route through the same six anchors:

- Fatherhood Under Pressure
- Relationship Breakdown and Betrayal
- Court / Custody Stress Without Losing Yourself
- Provider Identity and Work Loss
- Military / First Responder Transition
- PTSD / CPTSD and Survival-Mode Masculinity
- Loneliness and Rebuilding Brotherhood

These are not separate philosophies. They are different entry points into the same Masculine Restoration operating system.

## Video Format

A useful default format for member lessons, and a shorter public-preview version where appropriate:

1. **The pressure:** name the real situation plainly.
2. **The distinction:** clarify the idea without turning it into therapy-speak or macho theater.
3. **The pattern:** explain what can happen under pressure without diagnosing the viewer.
4. **The tool:** give one reflection, framework, or practical exercise.
5. **The action:** one thing the viewer can do within 24 hours.
6. **The route:** point to the relevant NBJB webpage/tool for deeper structured work.

## Shorts / Clips

Each long-form video can create several short clips built around one useful distinction rather than outrage bait.

Examples from Steadiness:

- Anger can carry information without getting command authority.
- Hypervigilance can protect you and still become expensive.
- You do not need perfect calm. You need enough traction to choose your next move.
- Control and agency are not the same thing.
- The skill that saved you may need an off switch.

Shorts should lead toward useful full content rather than deliberately keeping viewers angry for engagement.

## Website Integration

Do not publicly feature an empty YouTube channel simply because the URL exists.

Once a coherent public preview library and member learning library exist:

- add the YouTube channel to appropriate public discovery/social navigation,
- use public videos primarily for introductions, trailers, excerpts, and clips,
- route descriptions toward the relevant public page and Free Flamewalker signup,
- place full instructional videos inside the member Library / Watch experience,
- use consistent titles/thumbnails so viewers recognize the six-anchor system,
- keep paid-member and separately entitled program material out of public delivery.

## Behind-the-Curtain Content Ladder

The same core idea can move through multiple formats without becoming repetitive filler:

**Public webpage/tool → public trailer/clip → Free Flamewalker starter lesson → Paid Flamewalker deeper lesson/exercise → selected mini-course → workbook → flagship program → live workshop → speaking material**

Public content should remain useful by itself. The organized video instruction, larger exercises, worksheets, saved continuity, and guided progression begin inside membership rather than being published as the full public video library.

## First Release Sequence

A sensible first public YouTube sequence is:

1. Channel introduction: what NBJB is and is not.
2. Masculine Restoration trailer: the six anchors.
3. Steadiness preview / excerpt.
4. The Skill That Saved You May Need an Off Switch — public clip or preview.
5. Fatherhood Under Pressure — preview.
6. Relationship Breakdown — preview.

The corresponding full lessons should route into the Free or Paid Flamewalker learning library according to the access map. After that, build Direction and continue through the anchors while mixing in specialized branch previews.

## Safety / Scope Rules

YouTube content must preserve the same NBJB boundaries as the website:

- education and personal development, not therapy,
- lived experience is perspective, not clinical authority,
- no casual diagnosis of viewers, partners, ex-partners, parents, or public figures,
- no legal strategy presented as legal advice,
- no manufactured gender warfare,
- no spiritual certainty presented as fact,
- crisis content routes people toward appropriate emergency/professional support,
- do not use outrage, fear, dependency, loneliness, or trauma as pressure tactics for monetization.

## Success Metric

Do not judge YouTube only by subscribers.

Watch for:

- meaningful watch time,
- repeat viewers,
- comments showing understanding or application,
- traffic into the relevant NBJB pages,
- tool usage after videos,
- email/account conversion later,
- which anchors and specialized branches consistently pull interest.

That behavioral data can eventually tell NBJB which worksheets, mini-courses, workbook sections, workshops, and deeper programs deserve to be built first.
