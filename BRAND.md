# Not Broken Just Burning — Brand System

Version 0.3

## Core idea

**See clearly. Carry the fire differently. Build deliberately.**

Not Broken Just Burning is a survivor-led movement about turning lived experience into clarity, boundaries, identity, meaning, and deliberate rebuilding.

The brand should feel forged rather than polished, serious rather than gloomy, sacred without pretending every sentence is prophecy, and strong without confusing strength with aggression.

## Primary symbol: The Eye + The Flame

The NBJB sigil combines four visual elements:

- **Triangle** — structure, direction, discipline, and a container strong enough to hold heat.
- **Eye** — witness, memory, accountability, discernment, and the refusal to look away from reality.
- **Flame** — transformation, purification, energy, and the part of a person that survived the fire.
- **Radiance** — clarity moving outward. What is learned through survival can become light for someone else.

The Eye does not mean surveillance. The Flame does not mean destruction. Together they communicate:

> See what is true. Keep what is useful. Burn away what is false. Build what comes next.

Primary asset: `assets/sigil.svg`

## Community language

### Flamewalker

A **Flamewalker** is someone moving through the work of reclamation. The word does not imply rank, diagnosis, spirituality, or membership requirements. It is simply a movement identity for a person choosing to examine what happened and rebuild deliberately.

### Guardian

A **Guardian** is a future service and leadership identity within the movement, grounded in emotional discipline, protection of dignity, responsible boundaries, and the Guardian Code.

The public website should not imply that Guardian training, certification, paid mentorship, or professional services currently exist unless they have actually been created and launched.

## Guardian Code

- Trust
- Honor
- Respect
- Honesty
- Loyalty

These are behavioral filters, not demands for obedience.

## Six Sacred Questions

- Who?
- What?
- When?
- Where?
- Why?
- How?

Despite the name, these are practical investigative questions. They are used to move from emotional fog toward observable context.

## The six-stage path

1. Fire Reclamation
2. Guardian's Path
3. Sacred Mirror Work
4. Masculine Restoration
5. Voice of Fire
6. Circle of the Flame

The path is a map, not a hierarchy. A person may move between stages repeatedly.

- **Ember Orange:** `#ff7a2d`
- **Deep Ember:** `#d85f24`
- **Pale Ember:** `#ffad72`
- **Ash:** muted warm gray

Orange should be used as heat and emphasis, not as wallpaper.

## Typography

- **Display / ceremonial:** Cinzel
- **Body / functional:** Inter

Cinzel is for headings, symbols, short declarations, and identity language. Inter is for actual reading. Human beings have suffered enough without every paragraph being set like a medieval tomb inscription.

## Voice

Use:

- grounded intensity
- plain language
- forensic curiosity
- emotional honesty
- symbolic language when it adds meaning
- questions that lead toward agency
- strength paired with responsibility

Avoid:

- diagnosing strangers
- presenting survivor-originated frameworks as established science
- public accusations against identifiable private individuals
- revenge language
- promises of treatment or healing outcomes
- generic motivational sludge
- claiming spiritual interpretations are objective fact

## Publishing boundary

Private journals, therapy records, legal documents, books, manuscripts, paid curricula, unreleased products, and identifying information about other people are not public website content merely because they exist in connected storage.

Public content should extract lessons, tools, questions, frameworks, and personal reflections without turning private conflict into spectacle.
