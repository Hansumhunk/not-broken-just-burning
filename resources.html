Immediate danger · U.S.

Call 911.

If you or someone else is in immediate physical danger or facing a life-threatening emergency in the United States, use emergency services now rather than a reflection tool.

Emotional or suicide crisis · U.S.

Call or text 988.

The 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is available 24/7/365 by call, text, or official chat for emotional distress, mental-health or substance-use crisis, suicidal thoughts, or concern about someone else.

Open 988 Lifeline

Relationship abuse · U.S.

Domestic violence support.

National Domestic Violence Hotline: 800-799-SAFE (7233), text START to 88788, or use its official live chat and local-provider resources.

Open The Hotline

Outside the United States

Use local emergency and crisis services.

Emergency and crisis systems differ by country. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency service. For non-immediate support, use reputable local crisis, medical, mental-health, or domestic-violence resources.
