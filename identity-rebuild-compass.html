Values can outlast roles

Being a spouse, provider, employee or caregiver may have shaped your schedule and identity. When a role changes, it can feel like the whole person disappeared. Instead of demanding a completely new life plan tonight, identify one value you still want to embody—and one act that demonstrates it.

For example, 'integrity' can mean completing a small obligation without an audience. 'Connection' can mean checking in with a trusted friend without asking that person to solve your entire situation. Identity is rebuilt through repeated choices, not slogans.
