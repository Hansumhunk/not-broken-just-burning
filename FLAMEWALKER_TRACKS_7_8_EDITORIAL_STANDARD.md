## Non-negotiable word-count standard

- **Every new Track 7–8 lesson must include at least 3,000 substantive teaching words** — count only instructional paragraph content, not headings, titles, guided workbook prompts, footnotes, bibliography, captions, metadata, promotional text or repeated generic disclaimers.
- **Standard:** 3,000–3,500 teaching words, estimated 15–20 minutes' reading.
- **Deep Dive:** 3,500–4,500 teaching words, estimated 20–25 minutes' reading.
- **Flagship:** 4,500–5,500 teaching words, estimated 25–35 minutes' reading.
- Estimated reading times are approximate and exclude workbook completion. **A word-count pass is necessary but never sufficient.** A higher-tier lesson must reach its *assigned editorial target* unless the founder explicitly reclassifies it.
- **Existing Tracks 1–6 (35 lessons)** retain the previously established 2,500-word minimum and publication/review status; this approval does not alter the current published library.

## Substantive acceptance criteria for each lesson

- A distinct learning objective, connected to **Chaos → Clarity → Agency → Action → Integration**.
- Sufficient concept explanation to teach the **why**, not just a motivational statement or post.
- At least **two grounded, fictionalized worked scenarios**; vary the situation when possible (relationships, fatherhood, work, uncertainty, health/ordinary life) and show decision consequences.
- At least one meaningful counterexample, limitation, or misconception that helps prevent overgeneralization.
- A named, lesson-specific **step-by-step practice/tool**, one applied exercise, clear pitfalls and a realistic integration plan.
- Approximately 7–10 useful reflection prompts when appropriate, a **Next Honest Action**, a **Smaller Door**, and proportionate safety/professional-boundary guidance.
- Source-aware accuracy: distinguish firsthand description, factual records, inference, emotion, metaphor, empirical research, and unknowns. Do not convert private journals into publicly attributed diagnoses, findings, accusations, or evidence of supernatural phenomena.
- Trauma-informed men's education without exploitative fear, gender warfare, dependence on the creator, permanent victim identity, or claims to provide therapy.
- **Spiritual meaning-making must remain optional:** faith, prayer, ritual, awe, nature, values, symbolism and creativity are permissible paths; no demand to adopt supernatural claims. A symbol may clarify a choice but cannot independently prove someone's motives, a prediction, or destiny.
- No word padding, repetitive scripts, recycled case studies, or long disclaimers that substitute for actual teaching.

## Release acceptance stages

1. Draft privately, preserving source privacy; compare against the existing 35 and other proposed new lessons for redundancy.
2. Calculate actual substantive paragraph teaching word count; reject anything below **3,000**, and continue to the assigned tier/target.
3. Complete editorial source checks, differentiation, readability, worked examples, and practical application reviews.
4. Test the private reader, reflection tools, saving/resuming, keyboard/mobile usability, and real **paid/free/lapsed** entitlement controls.
5. Obtain **explicit founder signoff tied to each final revision**; only afterward perform a controlled publication and verify the actual customer-facing offering.
6. Keep AI video creation/publishing **shelved** unless separately authorized. This standard concerns **written lesson manuscripts** only.
