Privacy & Data Use

Your reflection should not become
somebody else's dataset.

NBJB is deliberately designed to collect less. Reflection tools stay separate from the member profile, and the current development member platform stores only a lightweight prototype snapshot on the visitor's own device.

What the reflection tools do

The Forge, Flame Check-In, Pattern Map, Boundary Builder, One Stone Planner, and Six Sacred Questions operate with client-side JavaScript. Their current behavior does not automatically send reflection entries to a member profile, NBJB database, analytics service, email platform, or AI model.

What the development Member Platform stores

The Flamewalker development experience uses browser localStorage for low-sensitivity prototype continuity. It can remember an optional display name, short profile text, profile visibility preference, current Path focus, Path progress states, one next honest action, selected Guardian Code values, the name of the last tool opened, onboarding status, and prototype member preferences. The current member key is nbjb.member.v2, with migration support from the earlier nbjb.member.v1 prototype.

What the Member Platform does not store

The member service is intentionally separate from the contents of private reflection tools. It does not automatically copy Forge notes, Pattern Maps, Six Sacred Questions answers, shame reflections, therapy or medical material, legal material, or other sensitive writing into the member profile.

The account screens are mockups

The development sign-in, create-account, and recovery screens are disabled workflow mockups. They do not create an account and intentionally do not accept real passwords. Real credential handling will be delegated to a reputable authentication provider when the backend bridge is connected.

Future account data should stay minimal

When real accounts launch, reasonable server-side data may include the authenticated member identifier, display name, Path focus/progress, selected low-sensitivity preferences, access entitlements, optional event/course progress, and operational timestamps. A next honest action should sync only if the member deliberately chooses that behavior. Private reflections should remain device-local by default unless a future secure-storage feature has an explicit purpose and consent flow.

What “copy” does

When you press a copy button, the site asks your browser to place generated text on your device clipboard. The site does not receive a copy of that text through the copy action.

Public email contact

NBJB can currently be contacted at notbrokenjustburning@gmail.com. If you choose to email that address, the message and ordinary email metadata are processed through Gmail/Google and may remain in the mailbox until intentionally deleted. Emailing NBJB does not enroll you in a newsletter or mailing list.

No live server-side member account or newsletter signup yet

The development Member Platform does not currently create an NBJB login or cloud profile. There is also no live newsletter database. Real member accounts and newsletter infrastructure will only be connected after provider choice, security rules, consent language, retention behavior, deletion/export options, unsubscribe behavior, and privacy implications are documented and tested.

No first-party analytics at launch

NBJB is launching without a first-party analytics or behavioral-tracking layer. The site does not currently add Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or a similar visitor-tracking script. If analytics are added later, this notice should be updated before or alongside that change.

Third-party requests

The current pages load web fonts from Google Fonts, so the browser may connect to Google's font infrastructure and transmit ordinary technical request information. Email links may open your chosen mail application. External links, including Facebook, crisis resources, and support resources, have their own privacy practices.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted with GitHub Pages at notbrokenjustburning.com. GitHub may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser details, request time, requested page, and security-related request data as part of operating and protecting the hosting service. NBJB does not receive reflection-tool entries or the device-local member snapshot through this hosting layer.

Do not enter or email secrets you cannot afford to expose

Browser-only tools, localStorage, and ordinary email are useful technologies, not magic armor. Shared devices, browser extensions, malware, screenshots, clipboard history, browser sync, email forwarding, device backups, or other local software can affect privacy. Avoid entering passwords, financial credentials, government identifiers, medical records, or legal evidence into prototype member fields.

NBJB privacy principles

Collect less. Explain more.

01

Do not collect sensitive reflection content merely because technology makes it possible.

02

When data collection becomes necessary, ask clearly and explain the purpose before collection.

03

Separate public/community participation from private therapeutic, medical, or legal material.

04

Give people realistic ways to clear local data, delete an account, export appropriate account data, unsubscribe, leave, or stop future sharing.

05

Do not treat a one-time email, account creation, or tool use as consent for unrelated future marketing or sensitive-data collection.

Current status

Privacy by limitation.

The member experience is becoming more capable without becoming more invasive. The eventual account bridge should preserve that boundary rather than using login as an excuse to collect everything a person writes.
