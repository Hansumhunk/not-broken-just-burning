Digital purchase terms — draft.

Not effective. Sandbox tests only. No live sale is authorized. The existing subscription terms and refund policy remain unchanged.

One-time purchase and delivery

Digital workbooks and toolkits are sold individually by Not Broken Just Burning LLC, once approved and released. The product page and checkout disclose the file format, price in USD and applicable tax. A free Flamewalker account is required; an ongoing paid membership is not. Your payment is verified before an owned product appears in My Library. Keep a backup of downloaded files. Re-download is offered while NBJB maintains the service and the purchase remains in good standing, not a guarantee of perpetual hosting. Corrections and updated editions are not guaranteed unless specified.

Personal use and limits

You may download and print purchases for your own personal use. No redistribution, resale, public posting, account sharing or circumvention of access controls; lawful exceptions remain. The material is general education and personal-development information, not diagnosis, therapy, crisis service or legal advice. H.A.W.K. is organizational support only; it does not determine legal deadlines, rights or outcomes.

Proposed refunds and payment disputes

Request a full first-purchase refund within 14 calendar days by contacting NBJB with the order reference and account email. Duplicate, unauthorized or undelivered purchases are reviewed promptly, and applicable consumer rights are preserved. A full refund ordinarily ends future download access; already saved copies cannot be remotely recalled. Partial refunds and disputed payments require individual review; disputed access may be temporarily held. Canceling a separate subscription does not cancel products you independently purchased. Refunds return to the original payment method. This policy does not change subscription refunds.

Proposed privacy addition

Supabase stores limited order and entitlement records: account identifier, SKU, payment references, amounts, tax/terms version, status and timestamps. Stripe processes checkout and payment details; NBJB does not store card numbers. Access links expire quickly and are not sent to analytics. Aggregate product views, checkout reservation counts and link issuance counts contain no account identifiers. Private reflections, legal documents and medical information are not requested for a purchase. Financial records may need retention under applicable law; account deletion and ownership recovery require a support process. The existing privacy policy otherwise applies.

Questions and support

Contact NBJB support. Do not email payment card information or sensitive case documents. Seller identity, final refund terms, retention duration, tax treatment and supported territories require approval before publication.
