Privacy boundary

Saving history is explicit.

Opening a tool does not automatically copy private writing into member history. A reflection is added only when the tool offers a clear Save to My Work action and you choose it. Optional themes, context, outcomes, what helped, and what changed are also member-entered. This prototype stores that history only on this device.

Future cloud design: member history should require a deliberate sync choice, deletion controls, and clear separation from highly sensitive therapy, medical, legal, or private journal material.
