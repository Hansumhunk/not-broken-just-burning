## Escalation / priority

- **P0** serious compromise, possible data exposure, broad customer-impact outage → human security/Executive Command; do not make changes automatically.
- **P1** verified paid access failure or urgent billing discrepancy → Revenue Forge, source-of-truth verification.
- **P2** ordinary account/navigation/technical/education question → Customer Experience, Infrastructure or Education based on affected feature.
- **P3** voluntary low-priority feature feedback → Customer Experience.
- **Immediate danger:** emergency/crisis resource guidance takes precedence, not routine ticketing.
- **Founder/human review:** refund exceptions, privacy/legal complaints, disputed account ownership and consequential actions; never promise a staffed response time without evidence.

Knowledge record fields for a future approved system: topic, audience, authoritative source, revision/timestamp, review owner, verification date, approved answer, expiration/review condition and escalation owner. Proposed changes to policy and customer rights require explicit human review.
