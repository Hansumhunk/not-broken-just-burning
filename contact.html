Before you email

Protect your own information.

Useful to send

  • your question or proposal
  • the page or tool involved
  • enough context to understand the request
  • a preferred reply address if different from the sender

Please do not send

  • passwords or financial credentials
  • Social Security or government ID numbers
  • private legal evidence dumps unless specifically requested
  • medical records or highly sensitive third-party information

Need urgent support instead?

Email can wait. Safety should not.

If the situation involves immediate danger or crisis, use the Support & Safety page for appropriate real-world resources.
