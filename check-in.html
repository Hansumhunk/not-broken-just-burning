This is the lighter daily tool. No diagnosis, no grand theory, no need to turn Tuesday afternoon into a doctoral dissertation. Notice what is happening and choose the next workable action.

Private by design: your entries stay in this browser session.

What this tool is for

A short emotional check-in when your head is crowded.

The Flame Check-In helps you slow down long enough to notice six separate things: what your body is doing, what emotion is present, which thought is loudest, whether a boundary is being crossed, what you actually need, and what action is realistic next.

It is intentionally smaller than a journal session. Use it when you feel overloaded, irritated, numb, scattered, or unsure what deserves attention first. The goal is not to solve the whole situation. It is to turn a vague internal storm into one honest snapshot you can work with.
