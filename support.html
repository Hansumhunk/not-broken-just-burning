Support & Safety

A reflection tool is not
an emergency service.

NBJB can help organize thoughts, identify next steps, and create language for reflection. It cannot monitor your safety, contact emergency services for you, diagnose conditions, or replace trained crisis, medical, mental-health, legal, or domestic-violence support.

Immediate danger

Call emergency services.

If you or someone else is in immediate physical danger or facing a life-threatening emergency in the United States, call 911.

Emotional or suicide crisis · U.S.

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988, or use the official 988 Lifeline chat. The service is available 24/7/365 for emotional distress, mental-health or substance-use crisis, suicidal thoughts, or when you are worried about someone else.

Open 988 Lifeline

Relationship abuse · U.S.

National Domestic Violence Hotline

Call 800-799-SAFE (7233), text START to 88788, or use the Hotline's official live chat for confidential support, safety planning, and local resources.

Open The Hotline

Outside the United States

Use local emergency and crisis resources.

Emergency numbers and crisis systems differ by country. If there is immediate danger, contact your local emergency service. For non-immediate support, use a reputable local crisis, medical, mental-health, or domestic-violence service.

What NBJB can do

Reflection, language, and next steps.

Use NBJB for

  • organizing a difficult thought
  • separating observation from inference
  • building a boundary around actions you control
  • breaking overwhelm into a smaller next action
  • reflecting on shame, identity, voice, and connection

Do not rely on NBJB for

  • crisis intervention
  • diagnosis or treatment
  • proof of abuse, motive, intent, or legal wrongdoing
  • emergency safety planning by itself
  • medical or legal decisions requiring a qualified professional

Use the right tool for the job

Reflection can wait when safety cannot.

If the situation is urgent, choose real-world support first. The Forge will still be here later. Human nervous systems, charmingly, do not care about our content roadmap.
