Paid-member advantage

Preferred pricing without flattening the product ladder.

Paid members can receive a defined discount or preferred price on eligible products. The percentage remains a pricing decision later; the entitlement architecture should support it now without permanently hard-coding one number.

Delivery rule

Purchase once. Find it again.

Successful checkout should create a product or event entitlement on the member account. The Owned Library then shows what that member can access.
