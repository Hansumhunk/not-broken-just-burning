Know what you're buying.

Draft for founder review only. This page is not an effective offer and does not authorize live checkout. Review accessibility, tax, cancellation and refund language before release.

Products, price and delivery

Not Broken Just Burning (NBJB) offers individually purchased downloadable educational workbooks and toolkits. The exact product title, edition, file type, USD price and any applicable transaction tax appear on the listing and Stripe payment page before payment. An account in free Flamewalker is required to retain ownership and retrieve downloads; Flamewalker+ paid membership is not required and is not included. A standalone purchase is not a subscription.

Purchased editions are delivered only after payment has been verified by our payment provider and recorded in your NBJB account. The owned product appears in My Library. Downloads use short-lived private links; do not share links with anyone else. Each product is an edition-specific ZIP containing the stated reading and print-edition PDF files. PDFs are non-fillable; you may print selected pages or complete exercises in a separate notebook. Nothing is shipped physically.

License and availability

A completed payment grants a nonexclusive, nontransferable right to use and print the specific purchased edition for personal use. It does not transfer copyright. Do not redistribute, resell, upload the files publicly, or share account credentials. Lawful exceptions remain intact. Keep a personal backup of the downloaded files. We intend to offer re-downloads while NBJB maintains the service and the order remains in good standing, but do not promise perpetual hosting, indefinite availability, or free future editions. If your account or file access fails, contact support with the order reference.

Refunds and payment issues — proposed

You may request a full refund of a first purchase of an individual edition within fourteen calendar days of the payment date through NBJB support. This is a proposed customer-friendly policy subject to founder approval. Duplicate charges, unauthorized transactions and paid-but-undelivered products will receive prompt review consistent with applicable law and provider protections. Refunds normally return to the original payment method, subject to the processor's timeline. A completed full refund ordinarily ends future access to that edition; files already downloaded cannot be recalled. Partial refunds and disputes are reviewed individually. Your unrelated product purchases and your separate Flamewalker+ subscription are unaffected unless expressly required by a specific resolution. No automatic cancellation or refund of a paid membership occurs when you return a workbook.

Education, safety and scope

These materials offer educational reflection, practical organization and personal-development exercises, not licensed therapy, medical diagnosis, mental-health treatment, legal advice, representation, custody advice or emergency assistance. The H.A.W.K. Complete Toolkit is an optional printable binder companion; its separate purchase does not restrict any of the existing free H.A.W.K. tools. Do not disclose private information about children, co-parents, legal cases or medical histories in a checkout or support message. For urgent safety or medical concerns, contact appropriate emergency or professional services rather than using these products as crisis assistance.

Data and payments

Stripe processes payment cards; NBJB does not request or store card numbers. NBJB's secured backend maintains the account identifier, purchased edition, order/price/tax/terms version, provider references, payment state, purchase date and refund status as needed to fulfill, support, audit and reconcile transactions. Short-lived private delivery links and ownership checks protect downloads. We do not require sensitive personal journals or case files to purchase a product. We retain some financial records where legally required; support can assist with account-access, privacy and deletion requests. Read our Privacy Policy for more information.

Geography, taxes and support

The initial standalone product offering is being prepared for customers in the United States. Availability and required tax collection must be configured and confirmed before the first sale; no statement here waives tax obligations. The final Checkout displays any calculated sales tax before the customer authorizes payment. If you have trouble opening a ZIP/PDF, accessing your product, correcting a purchase or requesting a refund, contact notbrokenjustburning@gmail.com or the support page. Do not email bank details, full card numbers or sensitive documents.
