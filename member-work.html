Privacy boundary

Nothing enters My Work just because you typed it.

A supported tool offers an explicit Save to My Work action, and you must confirm that choice. In this prototype, saved work remains on this browser/device. Future account sync should remain optional and clearly identify what will leave the device before it happens.

Future account rule: synced history should be separable from highly sensitive medical, therapy, legal, or private journal material, with deletion and export controls that actually work.
