Authentication bridge

Account security belongs to the auth provider.

The finished platform should delegate password handling, verification, recovery, sessions, and secure account identity to a reputable provider such as Supabase Auth. Service-role secrets do not belong in browser JavaScript.

Data boundary

What should stay local by default?

Keep local unless explicitly designed otherwise: Forge notes, Pattern Maps, Six Sacred Questions answers, shame reflections, sensitive journals, therapy/medical information, legal material, and similar private writing.

Reasonable future account data: authenticated member ID, display name, Path focus/progress, selected low-sensitivity preferences, access entitlements, course/event progress, and timestamps needed to operate the account.

Rule: creating an account must never silently convert private reflection into server data.

Account controls later

Deletion and export must be real, not decorative.

When the backend is connected, account deletion, data export, session management, and privacy controls need server-side behavior that matches the buttons the member sees.
