## Recommended order
1. Finish and approve the public workbook as a free trust-building resource; download via NBJB rather than requiring payment or an email address.
2. Finish one standalone book or workbook with distinct value and a completed file. Avoid a broad empty catalog.
3. Confirm business identity, sales-tax classification, legal/refund terms, support inbox, delivery method and the founder's price authorization before accepting orders.
4. Only with approval, activate a suitable Shopify plan and install/configure verified digital delivery; keep the product in draft while testing.
5. Test a sandbox or authorized test transaction, file receipt, correct product, order/refund behavior, accessibility, support and failure paths. No synthetic live purchases.
6. Obtain explicit release approval, then publish and add a clear website link. Do not change membership entitlements or create another subscription in Shopify.
