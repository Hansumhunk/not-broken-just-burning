Why one reliable ritual beats a perfect plan

Children do not need a complicated productivity system from a parent. They need adults who show up, explain practical changes appropriately and keep their word. A tiny routine works because you can repeat it even on an imperfect day.

Try choosing an existing anchor, such as putting shoes on, reading before bed or a permitted check-in while apart. Keep the focus on the child's life, not on whether the other adult approves.
