# Flamewalker+ — 2,500-teaching-word minimum completion audit

**Completed:** November 15, 2026  
**Scope:** The **28 previously unpublished** NBJB Flamewalker+ manuscripts (Tracks 2–6).  
**Source of record:** Protected Supabase `public.flamewalker_premium_lessons` manuscripts, not copied into GitHub.  
**Publication state:** 7 previously published founding lessons unchanged; all 28 expanded manuscripts remain `editorial_review` and private.

## Editorial standard

All 28 must contain **at least 2,500 teaching words**. The count is calculated from the manuscript's `content.blocks` entries where `type = "paragraph"`; it **does not include headings, separate exercise questions, titles, or metadata**. This is a strict threshold, not permission to lengthen a lesson with generic motivational filler.

The latest instructional expansion is the third round of detailed editing. The new teaching focuses on specific real-world decisions, counterexamples, duties and constraints, worked fictional cases, practical reflection techniques, and meaningful distinctions. Some already extensive material was retained, not needlessly rewritten.

## Verified outcome

- **28 of 28 private lessons at or above 2,500 words**
- Minimum: **2,502** teaching words; maximum: **2,648**; average: **2,576** (before this last pass the private draft average was approximately **1,998**)
- Private content state is unchanged: **28 `editorial_review`; 7 `published`**
- Structure checks: valid heading/paragraph blocks, nonempty safety and professional boundary material, 7–8 guided prompts per private lesson, meaningful Next Honest Action and Smaller Door, and 35 distinct exercise titles across the full curriculum
- Exact repeated guided questions across the 35-lesson library: **0**. Repeated long passages are limited to shared safety wording and source-provenance notes
- Closing order in the five shorter Placeholder Wound lessons verified; the practical 'One action' and 'Take this with you' sections now follow the full teaching, before the safety guidance
- Protected table row-level security remains enabled; `anon` and `authenticated` roles have no direct table `SELECT` grant. This is not a complete end-to-end entitlement test

## Release boundaries and remaining work

This audit **does not** authorize public promotion or release of the 28 review manuscripts. It does not change live prices, Stripe checkout, member entitlements, or website navigation.

Before release: (1) founder review and explicit editorial approval of the exact revision version of each lesson, including content quality, redundancy and sensitivity; (2) real-browser desktop and mobile paid/free/lapsed access checks, reader flow, exercise entry, keyboard usability and local save/resume; and (3) controlled publication with an appropriate audit of content availability and public marketing promises.

The existing seven published founding lessons **were not modified**. Their lengths are outside this specific 28-manuscript acceptance test; a library-wide standard would need a separate review of any founding lessons below 2,500.
