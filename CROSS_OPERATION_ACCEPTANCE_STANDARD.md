# NBJB cross-operation acceptance standard — v1.0

Purpose: a shared release gate, not permission to activate, publish, merge another operation's PR, send marketing, or spend money. The owning operation retains implementation and its own release control. Quality Control owns evidence classification. Use synthetic fixtures for private/personal data and test-only commerce.

## Common pass conditions
Every candidate identifies owner, base/commit SHA, intended environment, exact artifacts and feature-flag state, change scope, rollback point, and affected operations. Recheck mergeability against current main and rerun required CI after any candidate or dependency change. Evidence labels: PASS (specific executed check with timestamp/link); FAIL (reproducible defect); BLOCKED (missing environment/authority/credential); NOT RUN; N/A (with reason). A source inspection or mock pass does not establish live provider acceptance or a real browser journey. Verify production asset readback after an authorized release; prove access/privacy with two distinct QA identities and signed-out state when relevant. No credentials, private reflections, test customer IDs, real case data or payment references in public issues/CI.
Release blockers: unauthorized data disclosure, money/entitlement mismatch, destructive data loss, misleading product claims, missing consent/unsubscribe, broken delivery or recovery, failed required tests, missing approved rollback or ambiguous authority. Explicit founder signoff for production flag activation, financial provider/configuration changes, actual product publishing, public campaign sends and destructive migrations. Read-only proof and reviewed documentation are not deployment authorization. No extra scheduled tasks; use existing Weekly Operations Review for ongoing aggregate checks.

## Operation 03 — The Flame Letter (PR #230)
- Inactive flags and 31 draft campaigns/6 welcome messages remain locked until approved; verify correct sender and reply inbox with Op08, campaign links/landing pages and legal/disclosure copy.
- Consent: unticked explicit opt-in, bot protection, double opt-in, unsubscribe and suppression across all five lists; no signup/import or send without consent. Test duplicate, expired confirmation, resubscribe, invalid address, unsubscribe and suppression using internal authorized fixture list only. Never expose subscriber IDs or lists.
- Provider acceptance: approved sender domain, DKIM/SPF/DMARC, branded tracking, inbox placement, reply routing, consent records, scheduled limits (owner ceiling 240/day under free 300/day allowance). No active campaigns until founder approval. Product-related campaigns must wait for actual commerce fulfillment.

## Operation 09 — Native Digital Commerce (PR #242 / existing commerce staging)
- Five SKU manifest and exact approved price/currency/version/delivery file association, no client authority over price or product ownership. Test signed-in/out, malicious SKU/price, duplicate requests, concurrent checkout, wrong mode, unpaid/async payment, replay/invalid signatures, success redirect without webhook, refund/chargeback and entitlement independence. Test same member's membership and standalone product access separately.
- Existing Stripe sandbox only; never create another or send test events to a production-project endpoint. Separate approved Supabase QA project or isolated local stack; use only harmless mock PDF, sandbox credentials, two QA identities and no production keys. Check environment at every provider entry point.
- Verify expiry/reissue and ownership download denial, correct file/version/hash, private storage, no insecure public asset, refund policy, seller terms, tax decision, fulfillment/support. Sales and webhook deployment remain locked until evidence + explicit release approval.

## Digital Product Manufacturing (Operation 05)
- Verify manifest: versioned SKU, audience, title, price agreement, license, approved copy, final file hash, contents, page count and size, ownership rights, no private customer stories without consent, accessible text and appropriate educational/nonclinical positioning.
- Render all pages, visually inspect for clipping/contrast/readability, verify exercises match lesson content, no placeholders, malware or tracking; open and download from representative device. Test storefront delivery only with signed approved SKU mapping; synthetic assets are not commercial products. Founder approves final release inventory; no premature activation.

## SEO release candidates (PRs #237, #240)
- Required static/site diagnostics plus canonical URLs, valid sitemap/robots, unique titles/descriptions, structured data schema/visible-content alignment, broken-link and redirect checks, noindex on member/auth/private surfaces and no private content indexation.
- Compare repeatable desktop/mobile lab runs against same commit baseline, report median/spread and investigate regression rather than choosing fastest run. Verify production after approved rollout; distinguish search readiness from actual rankings/Search Console coverage. Keep unpublished content/private manuscripts out of public crawl.

## Membership and H.A.W.K.
- Free/Plus access matrix and protected endpoint authorization, checkout and cancellation, failed renewal expiry, signed receipt replay, refunds and separate product entitlements, local draft handoff, cross-account storage and export, reset/restore, mobile/keyboard accessibility, recovery email and support links. Inspect all published catalog slugs, sample each track with QA accounts; editorial acceptance separate.
- H.A.W.K.: enumerate all ten actual released tools; test blank/new/edit/delete/import/export, malformed/oversized file and schema/version, references between tools, data-loss confirmation, local/offline behavior and no unwanted outbound requests or analytics. Do not upload real legal documents, collect actual child identifiers, issue legal advice/deadlines, or claim court admissibility. Verify resource link, user instructions, device-local data risk and accessible keyboard/touch workflows. Do not duplicate H.A.W.K. implementation or mark completion without artifact-level evidence.

## Evidence handoff
Owner submits one record per gate (candidate SHA, environment, precise scenario, expected/observed result, PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED, redacted artifact/run URL, reviewer, time, remaining limitation and approval). Quality Control consolidates into the existing executive hub and issue trackers, not a new automation. Reopen on changed SHA/environment or unverified provider/user flow. All founder approvals must identify precise action and target.
