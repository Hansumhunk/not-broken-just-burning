# Flamewalker+ — All 35 lessons meet the 2,500-word teaching minimum

**Revision date:** November 16, 2026  
**Source of record:** Protected Supabase `public.flamewalker_premium_lessons` lesson bodies; manuscript bodies are not copied to GitHub.  
**Scope:** All 35 guided Flamewalker+ lessons.  
**Quality bar:** At least 2,500 words of substantive paragraph teaching per lesson, **excluding headings and separate guided worksheet questions and metadata**.

## Result

- **35/35 lessons** now meet the minimum.
- Overall verified paragraph word range: **2502–2929**; mean **2606**.
- The previously completed 28 unpublished draft manuscripts remain `editorial_review`; **none was promoted or exposed**.
- Five of the seven **already published** founding lessons were expanded in place in version 2, retaining original teaching and existing guided exercises. The other two published lessons were already above the minimum and were left at version 1.
- No change to payment prices, subscription eligibility, Stripe checkout, lesson slugs, navigation destination, public offers, or private lesson publication flags.
- User directed that **video work remain shelved**; this is exclusively a written-learning revision.

**Teaching additions**

- **The Father Wound:** the four-part separation of early observation from childhood inference, an example of work and parental presence, a three-generation decision map and a seven-day legacy experiment.
- **The Mother Wound:** adult choice without contempt, gratitude versus an unlimited obligation, mutual needs and communication, and a two-week practice.
- **The Boy Who Learned to Stay Quiet:** small truthful communication, over-explanation, a pause with a responsible return, and five concrete voice practices.
- **Bullying, Shame, and the Early Armor:** long-form exploration of defensive strategies; worked examples of silence, humor, intimidation and people-pleasing; a six-column shame-separation tool; seven-day armor experiment; and limits of disclosure and safety.
- **What You Learned You Were Allowed to Need:** need versus request, asking for defined help without mind-reading, responsible rest, and a one-week needs-permission practice.
