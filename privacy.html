Privacy & Data Use

Your reflection should not become
somebody else's dataset.

NBJB keeps private reflection local by default. Its interactive reflection tools run in your browser, while the free Flamewalker account uses Supabase for authentication, membership access, and limited account data.

What the current tools do

Public pages introduce The Forge, Flame Check-In, Pattern Map, Boundary Builder, One Stone, and Six Sacred Questions. Their working worksheets now run inside the free signed-in Flamewalker Toolbox. Entries and deliberately saved My Work remain on this browser/device unless a later feature explicitly offers and obtains consent for cloud synchronization. These reflection bodies are not sent to an NBJB database, analytics service, or email platform merely by using or saving a worksheet.

What “copy” does

When you press a copy button, the site asks your browser to place the generated text on your device clipboard. The site does not receive a copy of that text.

Public email contact

For privacy, personal-data and account-data requests, NBJB can be contacted at privacy@notbrokenjustburning.com. If you choose to email that address, inbound mail is forwarded to NBJB's central Gmail inbox, where the message and ordinary email metadata are processed through Gmail/Google and may remain in the mailbox until intentionally deleted. Emailing NBJB does not enroll you in a newsletter or mailing list.

Flamewalker account data

Free Flamewalker accounts use Supabase for authentication and membership authorization. Account data may include your email address, authenticated user ID, membership entitlement, and limited low-sensitivity account or progress fields needed to operate the member experience. Private reflection bodies are not automatically uploaded merely because you create or sign into an account.

Account deletion and export

Members can request server-side account deletion from their settings page, and may download a limited authenticated account-data JSON export from Settings. The export includes account, profile, preferences, server progress, continuity and access records; it excludes Stripe receipts and device-local writing, which have separate controls. The downloaded file is unencrypted. Self-service account deletion and full administrative data export are not available. Until those controls are available in the member interface, account-removal requests can be sent to privacy@notbrokenjustburning.com. Clearing device-local member data does not by itself delete the authenticated account.

H.A.W.K. offline case-organization tools

The public teaching pages follow the website analytics policy below. The separately downloaded Incident Timeline Builder, Evidence Index, Documentation Checklist, Communication Analyzer, Court Preparation Organizer, Parenting Log, Financial Organizer, Witness Observation Organizer, Support Network Map and H.A.W.K. Dashboard are self-contained HTML applications with no analytics, network connections, account integration or automatic storage. Incident, evidence index, checklist entries, communication drafts, preparation entries, parenting notes, financial entries, witness observation notes, support planning entries and dashboard snapshots stay in memory until you explicitly export a file; closing or reloading the tool can lose unsaved work. Import reads a file on your device, not an upload to NBJB. The Evidence Index can optionally read a timeline JSON export and retain event identifiers in memory for reference checks; it does not upload or modify the timeline. The Documentation Checklist can also read timeline and evidence JSON exports, retaining only identifier sets for reference checks. Neither source file is modified. The checklist does not transmit requests, calculate legal deadlines or provide notifications. The Communication Analyzer uses local literal text rules, not external AI, and does not send messages. Its optional JSON export includes draft text, decision choices and checklist responses; no sent-message record is created. The Court Preparation Organizer optionally reads earlier timeline, evidence index and checklist exports, retaining only reference identifier sets in memory; it does not alter source files, generate filings or calculate legal deadlines. The Parenting Log uses child aliases and minimal text notes, and can optionally read timeline, evidence and checklist identifiers locally. It does not accept attachments, track people, calculate custody time or assess a child's health. The Financial Organizer uses manually entered amounts and source references; it never connects to financial institutions, requests credentials or produces legal entitlement calculations. The Witness Observation Organizer uses aliases, attributed notes, source limitations and optional local reference checks. It does not contact sources, solicit statements, generate sworn documents or score credibility. The Support Network Map uses resource aliases, support needs, availability, boundaries and ordinary fallback planning; it does not maintain a private contact directory, contact anyone, monitor safety or dispatch assistance. The H.A.W.K. Dashboard can read complete local version-one exports and selected workspace files, validate their structures and retain them in memory; it does not edit originals or merge records. Communication and support modules require explicit import permission and are excluded from combined exports by default. Private action details are hidden until requested; printed summaries contain counts and reference totals, not record text. Workspace files contain all selected module data and are unencrypted.

Exported JSON and printed copies are not encrypted by the tool. Choose secure storage and avoid shared or monitored devices when appropriate. Files you export or print are under your control; NBJB cannot recover, erase or protect copies you save separately. Preserve original records securely and follow applicable professional preservation instructions.

Minimal cookieless analytics

NBJB uses PostHog for basic aggregate website analytics, including public pageviews, anonymous free membership link clicks, and eligible checkout request counts by monthly or annual plan. Checkout intent does not establish payment or identify a person. Private member workspaces and account access/recovery pages are excluded from analytics. The production site is configured in cookieless mode with no persistent browser identifier. NBJB does not enable session replay, heatmaps, click autocapture, console-log capture, automatic exception capture, or person identification. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals are honored by not loading the analytics script.

What analytics does and does not include

The analytics setup records ordinary pageview information needed for aggregate traffic reporting, such as the page/path requested, time of the visit, referrer, bounded campaign tags, and basic browser/device context supplied with the web request. URL query strings and fragments are removed from recorded URL properties; pages with unexpected query parameters or token-like fragments do not initialize analytics. PostHog uses privacy-preserving cookieless processing for visitor counts, and the project is configured not to retain raw client IP addresses. The contents you type into The Forge, Flame Check-In, Pattern Map, Boundary Builder, One Stone Planner, or other reflection fields are not sent to PostHog.

Third-party requests

The current pages load web fonts from Google Fonts and, unless your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the PostHog analytics library from PostHog's infrastructure. Flamewalker account pages also connect to Supabase for authentication and authorized member data. Those services receive ordinary technical request information necessary to provide their functions. Email links may open your chosen mail application. External links, including Facebook, crisis resources, and support resources, have their own privacy practices.

Hosting and technical logs

The site is hosted with GitHub Pages at notbrokenjustburning.com. GitHub may process routine technical information such as IP address, browser details, request time, requested page, and security-related request data as part of operating and protecting the hosting service. NBJB does not receive reflection-tool entries through this hosting layer.

Do not enter or email secrets you cannot afford to expose

Browser-only tools and ordinary email are useful communication methods, not magic armor. Shared devices, browser extensions, malware, screenshots, clipboard history, email forwarding, device backups, or other local software can affect privacy. Avoid entering or emailing passwords, financial credentials, government identifiers, medical records, or other high-risk secrets unless there is a specific and appropriate reason.

NBJB privacy principles

Collect less. Explain more.

01

Do not collect sensitive reflection content merely because technology makes it possible.

02

When data collection becomes necessary, ask clearly and explain the purpose before collection.

03

Separate public community participation from private therapeutic or legal material.

04

Give people a realistic way to unsubscribe, leave, or stop sharing future data.

05

Do not treat a one-time email as consent for future marketing.
