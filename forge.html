The Forge

Put the thought
under heat.

Strong emotions deserve respect, but they are not automatically evidence. Use the Forge to slow the thought down, test it, and rebuild it into something more accurate and useful.

🔒 Your Forge draft is saved only in this browser on this device. It is not submitted to NBJB, an AI service, or a server.

Step 1

Name what is burning.

The Six Sacred Questions

Who is involved? What happened? When did it happen? Where did it happen? Why do I think it matters? How do I know?

Step 2

Ask the Forge to analyze what you wrote.

This first version uses transparent, rule-based analysis inside your browser. It does not decide who is right, diagnose anyone, verify allegations, or determine legal truth. It looks at how you separated facts, assumptions, supporting and challenging evidence, patterns, predictions, motive claims, and the standard you would use for someone you care about.

On-device analysis only. Nothing you type here is sent to an AI model or NBJB server.

Forge Analysis

What the Forge sees in your reasoning.

This is a structured reflection, not a truth score. Its job is to show you where your thinking is grounded, where uncertainty remains, and what deserves another question.

Step 3

A boundary worth keeping

This is reflection, not crisis care.

If a thought involves immediate danger, self-harm, harming someone else, or a medical emergency, use real-world emergency or professional support rather than trying to reason your way through it alone.
