Founding library · Seven lessons published

Thirty-five guided lessons across six tracks.

Track 1: The Wounded Boy and Inherited Manhood contains seven full-length lesson manuscripts and guided exercises. Track 2: The Placeholder Wound contains eight relationship recovery modules, each with practical exercises. Track 3: When the Safe Room Becomes the Courtroom adds five lessons about trust, helping professionals, credibility, and accountability. Track 4: Steadiness & Emotional Command adds five on body care, emotion, restraint, boundaries and habits. Track 5: Rebuilding a Life That Holds adds five on work, fatherhood, brotherhood, leadership and integration. Track 6: The Five Sacred Laws of Survival adds five on emotional boundaries, pattern recognition, self-worth, strategic retreat and the authentic voice. Seven founding lessons are published in the protected library; the remaining twenty-eight are editorial drafts. Paid enrollment remains closed pending independent entitlement, device, payment, and cancellation testing.

Track 2 · The Placeholder Wound (eight expanded lessons): Being Needed Is Not Being Chosen; The Love Bargain; Red Flags and Responsibility; The Betrayal Rewrite Trap; Children Are Not Relationship Glue; Loyalty Without Self-Abandonment; Grieving the Future That Never Happened; and The Other Person Is Not Your Measure. All include guided reflection and a Next Honest Action. The original shorter public educational articles remain available for future free use.

Track 3 · When the Safe Room Becomes the Courtroom (five expanded lessons): The Promise of the Room; Split Alliance; The Professional Authority Multiplier; When You Stop Trusting Your Own Account; and The Double Bind, DARVO, and Reaction as Evidence. These are educational tools, not clinical or legal findings; reference checks and founder review are required before publication.

Track 4 · Steadiness & Emotional Command (five lessons): Your Body Is Not the Enemy; Name the Emotion, Choose the Action; The Space Between Trigger and Response; Boundaries Without Cruelty; Discipline Without Self-Punishment.

Track 5 · Rebuilding a Life That Holds (five lessons): Provision Without Proving Your Worth; Fatherhood Under Pressure; Brotherhood Without Dependency; Lead Yourself Before Leading Others; The Integrated Man: A Thirty-Day Rebuilding Map.

The ten lessons in Tracks 4 and 5 were developed from the NBJB Masculine Restoration foundation architecture and still require formal editorial review.

Track 6 · The Five Sacred Laws of Survival (five lessons): Emotional Boundaries; Pattern Recognition; Sacred Self-Worth; Silence and Strategic Retreat; and the Authentic Voice. Their core laws are recovered from NBJB's original five-law guide and rebuilt through the later second-edition framework, with new guided practice. The separate book manuscript remains under revision.

The lessons are educational resources, not psychotherapy. No live videos or future content volume is promised. The final founding offer will specify exactly which approved lessons are available on the purchase date.
