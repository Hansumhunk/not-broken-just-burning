The Guardian Code · 04

Honesty is more than
saying what you feel.

Honesty requires courage, but it also requires precision. A feeling can be true as a feeling without every conclusion attached to it being a fact.

Why honesty matters

Confusion grows when facts, assumptions, feelings, memories, and motives are blended into one sentence. Honesty slows that mixture down.

In NBJB, honesty means trying to represent reality as accurately as you can, including the parts that are inconvenient to your preferred story.

Truth becomes stronger when it can survive clarification.

What honesty is not

Honesty is not “brutal honesty” used as permission to be cruel. It is not oversharing every private detail. It is not declaring certainty where you only have suspicion. It is not using one technically true fact to create a misleading overall picture.

Telling the truth includes context, proportion, and the willingness to say I do not know.

Separate the layers

A useful discipline is to separate four things: what happened, what you felt, what you think it means, and what you still do not know.

For example: “The message was not answered” is different from “I felt rejected,” which is different from “They ignored me because they do not care.” The first is an observation. The second is an internal experience. The third is an interpretation that may or may not be supported.

Honesty includes revision

If better evidence appears, honesty allows the story to change. That can feel humiliating when identity has become attached to being right, but revision is not betrayal of the truth. It is loyalty to it.

Honesty also applies inwardly. Sometimes the uncomfortable truth is that you are hurt. Sometimes it is that you contributed to the problem. Sometimes it is that you want something you are afraid to admit. Clear seeing begins by allowing those facts onto the table.

Guardian practice

Use four columns.

For one difficult situation, write: Fact · Feeling · Interpretation · Unknown. Do not let a statement move columns unless the evidence supports it.
