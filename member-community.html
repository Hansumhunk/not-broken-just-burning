Community model

Website for structure. Discord for live conversation.

A useful split is to keep member identity, progress, official resources, programs, and structured discussion prompts on the NBJB platform while Discord handles fast conversation, live rooms, announcements, and community energy.

The two can be linked later, but neither should become a place where private legal, medical, therapy, or identifying material gets casually turned into public entertainment.

Before launch

The boring rules are what keep the interesting community alive.

Community launch still requires clear moderation roles, reporting and blocking, age policy, privacy defaults, anti-harassment and anti-doxxing rules, crisis boundaries, and limits around members presenting medical or legal advice as professional guidance.
