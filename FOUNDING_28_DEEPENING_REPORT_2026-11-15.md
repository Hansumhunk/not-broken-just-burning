## Result

- **28/28 private manuscripts** received additional, lesson-specific teaching in this second pass. Five tracks are represented.
- All 28 now range from **1,854 to 2,528 teaching words**, averaging **1,998 words** (paragraph text only; headings, separate guided prompts and metadata excluded). This compares with approximately 1,381 words per private draft in the earlier audit, measured with a similar paragraph-only method.
- The additions focused on genuine instructional value: nuanced distinctions, counterexamples, detailed fictional case studies, real-world constraints, proportionate decisions, repeatable exercises and follow-through.
- The three already long Placeholder Wound lessons each received an advanced layer instead of receiving filler for length.
- Five shorter Placeholder Wound lessons now place their **One action** and **Take this with you** closures after the expanded teaching, just before the safety note.
- User-requested video work remains shelved. **No video release** is implied by manuscript progress.

## Evidence/source confirmation

The existing evidence-sensitive Safe Room research notes were checked against the actual source titles and DOIs:

- Friedlander, Escudero, Welmers-van de Poll, & Heatherington (2018), *Meta-analysis of the alliance–outcome relation in couple and family therapy*, `10.1037/pst0000161` — group-level meta-analysis, not a verdict about a particular clinician.
- Harsey & Freyd (2020), *Deny, Attack, and Reverse Victim and Offender (DARVO): What Is the Influence on Perceived Perpetrator and Victim Credibility?*, `10.1080/10926771.2020.1774695` — experimental-vignette research, not an individual-case determination.
- Drożdżowicz & Grodniewicz (2025), *Epistemic injustice and psychotherapy*, `10.1080/09515089.2025.2519320` — philosophical conceptual analysis, not a diagnosis or finding of professional misconduct.
