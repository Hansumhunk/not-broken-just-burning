Privacy boundary

Nothing enters My Work just because you typed it.

Each supported tool offers an explicit Save to My Work action, and you must confirm that choice. Optional comparison context is saved only with that entry. Edits remain on this browser/device in the current prototype. Future account sync should remain optional and clearly identify what will leave the device before it happens.

Future account rule: synced history should be separable from highly sensitive medical, therapy, legal, or private journal material, with deletion and export controls that actually work.
