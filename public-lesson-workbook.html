Public lesson companion workbook

Fourteen independent printable worksheets. Start with whichever lesson is useful today. You don't need to finish every page or disclose anything to NBJB.

Draft for review. This is a paper-based or print-to-PDF companion to public teaching, not a replacement for signed-in interactive tools. No online input, account, submission or saved reflection is implemented on this page.

Print using your browser's Print command (Ctrl+P or Command+P). Select individual page ranges or print all 14. Preview before printing; paper size and browser settings can change pagination. Write on paper or in your own private document.

For immediate danger or crisis, use Support & Safety before reflecting. See Privacy & Data Use.

Clarity Before Certainty

Separate what is known from what is inferred.

Read the companion lesson

Choose a manageable situation or a fictional example. Skip any prompt that does not help. Keep your notes somewhere safe.

1. Write one observation without assuming intent.

2. Identify your interpretation and one prediction. Label what remains unknown.

3. What evidence could change your view? What can safely be done with the facts already available?

Next honest action

Choose one proportionate action, or a reason to wait.

Boundaries Are Actions

Read the companion lesson

Choose a manageable situation or a fictional example. Skip any prompt that does not help. Keep your notes somewhere safe.

1. Identify one behavior and the need or value it affects.

2. Identify a proportionate response within your control, including safety or existing obligations.
