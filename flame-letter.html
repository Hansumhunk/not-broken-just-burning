Sample edition · Clarity Before Certainty

You do not need every answer to take the next honest step.

When life becomes uncertain, the mind wants a complete explanation. But urgency is not evidence. You can acknowledge that something hurt without deciding what every person intended.

One question: What do I know today, and what am I still trying to figure out?

One tool: Use the Six Sacred Questions to organize the situation. Mark an unanswered question as unknown rather than forcing an answer.

One action: Write down three observable facts and choose one task within your control. Leave the rest for another day.

— Rickey Partin Jr., founder
