Why It Exists

Built from lived experience. Aimed beyond one life.

Not Broken Just Burning grew from trauma recovery, fatherhood, journaling, pattern-mapping, spiritual meaning-making, and the search for truth when the old story stopped making sense.

The founding mission was to awaken, guide, and protect survivors by turning pain into power. Today that idea is expressed carefully: helping people strengthen agency, boundaries, discernment, voice, and the ability to choose what they build next.

The purpose is not to build a monument to pain or turn private conflict into public spectacle. It is to extract what is useful: language, questions, boundaries, stories, and tools that help other people reclaim authorship of their own lives.

— Rickey Partin Jr.
