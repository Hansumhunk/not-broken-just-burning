Before you decide

Know what you're joining.

Can I buy Flamewalker+ today?

No. All thirty lessons are still undergoing editorial review. You can create a free Flamewalker account today, but paid checkout is not enabled.

Are videos, AI Doc Maker narration, or live groups included?

No. The current founding plan is for written self-guided lessons and their exercises. The incomplete AI Doc Maker voiceovers are not offered. Video series, groups, and live events remain separate future possibilities, not launch benefits.

Is this therapy or personal legal advice?

No. NBJB provides education, reflection tools, and lived-experience perspectives. It does not replace licensed mental-health care, medical treatment, legal representation, or emergency support. You can take breaks or skip prompts that do not fit your situation.

Will my private reflections follow me between devices?

Not currently. When you explicitly save your writing to My Work, it is stored locally in that browser on that device. It is not automatically uploaded, encrypted, or synchronized to another phone or computer. Use the Copy option if you need your own backup.

How are the paid lessons different from public NBJB articles?

Public education remains useful and accessible. The proposed paid experience adds long-form teaching, structured scenarios, guided reflection, and practical tools across three distinct learning tracks.

No payment required to start

Start free, see how the Flamewalker tools work, and decide later whether the deeper learning track is worth your money. Trust should come before a transaction.
